Why Nostu Wavzz?

Radio, the way it was meant to be.

No accounts. No algorithms deciding what you hear. No ads interrupting your favorite song. Just you, the dial, and the whole world.

The whole world, on a globe

Spin the Earth, watch it glow with stations from 188 countries. Tap any dot and hear what's playing right now — live from Tokyo, Lagos, Buenos Aires, or a village you've never heard of.

50,000+ stations, always live

From talk radio in Berlin to jazz in New Orleans, from Bollywood beats in Mumbai to ambient sounds in Reykjavik. Every station, live and direct.

Your station, always ready

Every station cached locally on your device, with a one-tap Recently Played row waiting for you. Your favorites, always a tap away — even without internet.

Uninterrupted vibes

Spotty connection? Bad signal? Nostu Wavzz silently retries until your stream comes back. Because nothing kills the mood like a dead station.

Fall asleep to the world

Set a sleep timer and drift off to the sound of a distant city. When the time comes, the volume gently fades out before it stops — like falling asleep to the real thing.

Find your station in a flash

Search 50,000 stations by name, country, language, or genre in milliseconds — and search the app's settings too. Press Ctrl+K and find exactly what you're looking for.

Controls on your lock screen

Play, pause, and switch stations right from your phone's notification shade or lock screen — even while the app runs in the background. Pauses politely when a call comes in, and picks back up when you reconnect your headphones or Bluetooth.

Seamless station switching

Swipe between stations in the bottom-sheet player with a buttery crossfade (adjustable from 0.5 to 3 seconds). No dead air, no clicks — the music just keeps flowing.

10-band equalizer & audio FX

Full parametric EQ with presets, bass boost, spatial audio widening, and night mode compression. Shape the sound exactly how you like it — plus a real-time waveform visualizer.

Light & dark themes

A beautiful light mode for daytime and a deep, dark mode for nights — plus a pure-black AMOLED option. The app even tints itself with the artwork of whatever station you're playing.

Android only (for now)

Start listening in seconds.

No Google Play needed. Just download, install, and rediscover radio.

  1. Download the APK

    Tap the download button above. The file is 18 MB and works on Android 7.0+.

  2. Allow unknown sources

    Chrome will prompt you to allow installs. The setting is stored per-app, so it only affects this APK.

  3. Open and install

    Tap the downloaded file in your notifications, then tap Install. First launch will sync the 50K station catalog.
