Why Nostu Wavzz?

Radio, the way it was meant to be.

No accounts. No algorithms deciding what you hear. No ads interrupting your favorite song. Just you, the dial, and the whole world.

The whole world, on a globe

Spin the Earth, watch it glow with stations from 188 countries. Tap any dot and hear what's playing right now — live from Tokyo, Lagos, Buenos Aires, or a village you've never heard of.

50,000+ stations, always live

From talk radio in Berlin to jazz in New Orleans, from Bollywood beats in Mumbai to ambient sounds in Reykjavik. Every station, live and direct.

Your station, always ready

Every station cached locally on your device. Search, browse, and discover — even without internet. Your favorites, always a tap away.

Uninterrupted vibes

Spotty connection? Bad signal? Nostu Wavzz silently retries until your stream comes back. Because nothing kills the mood like a dead station.

Fall asleep to the world

Set a sleep timer and drift off to the sound of a distant city. Radio has always been the best lullaby — now it comes with a timer.

Find your station in a flash

Search 50,000 stations by name, country, language, or genre in milliseconds. Press Ctrl+K and find exactly what you're looking for.

Android only (for now)

Start listening in seconds.

No Google Play needed. Just download, install, and rediscover radio.

  1. Download the APK

    Tap the download button above. The file is 17.8 MB and works on Android 7.0+.

  2. Allow unknown sources

    Chrome will prompt you to allow installs. The setting is stored per-app, so it only affects this APK.

  3. Open and install

    Tap the downloaded file in your notifications, then tap Install. First launch will sync the 50K station catalog.

Security note: Signed with our release key and verified with apksigner verify. Same signature means smooth updates. Always download from the official GitHub link above — never from third-party mirrors.
